# Personality, Interests and Career Aspirations

## Personality

I am interested in learning new things, especially when they relate to technology. However, I sometimes procrastinate when starting something new.

I can spend a long time thinking about trying a new game before actually deciding to play it.

I prefer natural conversations and straightforward communication.

## Hobbies

I enjoy playing video games and watching online videos.

Gaming is one of my main interests.

## Career Aspirations

My preferred career direction is game development. I would love to become a game developer, although I have not found enough opportunities to explore game development through my university.

I am also considering mobile application development and web development as possible career paths.

I am interested in continuing to learn and improving my practical development skills.

## Communication Preferences

I prefer casual and friendly communication in everyday conversations.

When discussing professional matters, academic work, internships or career-related topics, I prefer a more professional communication style.

I like explanations that are clear, natural and easy to understand.
