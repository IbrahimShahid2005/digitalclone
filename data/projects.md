# My Software Projects

## Game Hub

Game Hub is a gaming platform that offers a variety of games. Users can play games against the computer or enjoy playing with their friends.

The project was developed collaboratively with a teammate. We both contributed to backend development because the platform included multiple games.

The technologies used were JavaScript and React.

This project gave me experience working collaboratively on a software development project involving multiple games.

## AI Content Generator

My AI Content Generator is my ongoing Final Year Project.

The goal is to create a web-based platform that combines different creative tools in one place.

The platform aims to make content creation and editing easier for users. Instead of switching between different resources, users will be able to access multiple creative capabilities through one platform.

Planned areas include AI content generation and video editing.

The project is currently under development. I expect to complete it by the end of my eighth semester.

The final technology stack has not yet been decided.
